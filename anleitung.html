you want to be a fortunate winner?

"Fortunate Finalists" is a simple elimination game of chance designed to decide everyday tasks—like who does the dishes or takes out the trash—while revealing your fate one round at a time.

1. setup

  • Pick 2-4 players.
  • Place your device in the center; each player claims one screen edge.
  • click the "play" button to start the game.

2. play

  • Shuffle: Cards spin endlessly. Tap the screen anytime to stop.
  • Reveal: One card per player flips.
  • Eliminate: The Red Card (lowest fate) is out! That player leaves the table.

3. win

  • Repeat until only two players remain.
  • The survivor is the Fortunate Finalist.
  • Losers: Agree on your own punishment!
  • Click "play again" to start a new game.
